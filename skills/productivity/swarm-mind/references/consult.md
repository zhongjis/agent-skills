# Consult

Applies [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) to an open question, such as a repo audit or a design question, rather than a change or a plan.

- **Target:** the question plus a pinned snapshot (the repo at a commit, or named paths).
- **Authority:** the question's criteria, then the repo's own rules (`AGENTS.md`, ADRs).
- **Output:** findings ranked by impact, each with its location and evidence; no blocking verdict and no later rounds unless the caller asks.
